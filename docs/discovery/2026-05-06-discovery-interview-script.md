# OwnVoice — Discovery Interview Script and Probe Inventory

**Phase:** 90-day discovery and validation (Day 1 to Day 90)
**Last updated:** 2026-05-06
**Companion document:** `docs/strategy/2026-05-06-business-strategy.md`

---

## 1. Goals of the discovery phase

The 90-day discovery phase tests three load-bearing hypotheses that gate every subsequent commitment (incorporation, equity grants, IRB protocol, STTR drafting):

1. **H-Existential:** voiceless-ICU-patient communication is a *funded* category in current US ICU procurement — not just a documented pain.
2. **H-HRO:** the institutional-safety / HRO / Joint Commission / ICU Liberation Bundle / CMS Health Equity framing unlocks budget conversations that pure patient-experience framing does not.
3. **H-Procurement:** the unit-level credit-card-discretionary buying motion is real (vs. forced into central procurement / FDA-clearance gating).

Secondary hypotheses tested through the same conversations:

- **H-Voice-Quality:** the on-device + cloud-premium voice-cloning architecture produces clinically-credible results from real-world poor reference audio (voicemails, social media clips, old recordings).
- **H-Buyer-Persona:** the unit director / palliative care lead / nursing director is the right point of accountability to sell to.
- **H-Co-Founder:** a Boston palliative care or critical care attending will engage with OwnVoice as a clinical co-founder candidate.

## 3. Pre-interview prep

Before each conversation:

- **Read 2–3 recent papers / interviews / hospital announcements involving the interviewee.** Cite something specific in the opening; demonstrates you're not cold-pitching.
- **Confirm the conversation is exploratory, not a demo.** Set expectation: "I'd love to learn how your unit handles voiceless-patient communication today, and what's actually painful about it. I have a 60-second product demo I can show at the end if it's useful."
- **Pre-fill the post-interview memo template** with name / role / institution / scheduled topic.
- **Have the 60-second voice-clone demo loaded on iPad** with 3–5 different reference samples ready (varying quality: clean read-speech, voicemail-quality, social-media-quality, multilingual). Don't open the iPad until requested.

---

## 4. Interview structure (45–60 minutes)

| Time | Phase | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Opening, context, rapport | Set expectation, light context |
| 5–25 min | **Behavioral probes** — what's painful, what's currently done, what gets funded, who owns the problem | Test H-Existential and H-HRO without leading |
| 25–35 min | **Procurement and budget probes** | Test H-Procurement |
| 35–45 min | **Demo (only if asked, or in last 15 min)** | Test H-Voice-Quality reaction |
| 45–55 min | **Co-founder / collaboration probe (palliative care + intensivists only)** | Test H-Co-Founder |
| 55–60 min | **Close — referrals + follow-up + permission to revisit** | Build pipeline |

Critical rule: **demo only after behavioral probes are done.** The temptation is to demo early because it lands emotionally; resisting it for 25 minutes is the difference between measuring real demand and measuring polite enthusiasm.

## 5. Probes by hypothesis

### Probes for H-Existential (is it actively-funded pain?)

These come *first*, before any product context is shared. The goal is to detect unprompted mention of voiceless-patient communication as a priority.

- "What are your unit's / department's top 3 quality or patient-experience priorities this fiscal year?"
- "Tell me about a recent shift that didn't go well. What broke?"
- "When was the last time someone on your team said 'we should buy something for X'? What was X?"
- "Walk me through the last time a non-speaking patient had a communication breakdown on your unit. What happened? What did the team do?"
- "If a nurse on shift identifies that an intubated patient is suffering from communication failure, who do they escalate to?" *(Listen for: "no one in particular" — that's the accountability gap.)*
- "What did your unit decide *not* to buy this year, in patient-experience or AAC?"
- "Has anyone in the past 12 months evaluated VidaTalk, CommuniCare, or any AAC tool here? What happened?"
- "What does success look like for your unit in 12 months? Would solving voiceless-patient communication move that needle?"

### Probes for H-HRO (does institutional-safety framing unlock budget?)

- "Where is your hospital in its HRO journey, and what's funded against it this year?"
- "Who owns communication-failure analysis when it shows up in a sentinel event review?"
- "What's your unit's plan for ICU Liberation A2F Bundle compliance, especially the 'F' (Family engagement) element?"
- "How do you currently measure or address LEP-patient outcome disparities in your ICU?"
- "When the Joint Commission survey team asks about voiceless-patient communication, what do you show them?"
- "Has anyone in patient safety / quality tied communication failure with non-speaking patients to a specific safety event or near-miss?"

### Probes for H-Procurement (does the unit-level buying motion work?)

- "When you've purchased communication tools (AAC, translation, language services) in the past, what budget did that come out of? Who approved? What was the typical magnitude?"
- "What's the largest expenditure your unit director can authorize without going to central procurement?"
- "Has your unit ever purchased a software tool on a discretionary credit-card basis and expensed it through the unit budget? Tell me about that."
- "What's your hospital's posture on procuring software tools that aren't FDA-cleared? Is that a procurement-blocker or a discussion item?"
- "If you wanted to pilot a $3,000-a-year software tool on your unit for a year, what's the path from 'I want this' to 'we have it'?"
- "Who at your hospital owns the contract review for software vendors? What's their typical turnaround?"

### Probes for H-Voice-Quality (does the demo land emotionally?)

After running the 60-second demo:

- "What's your reaction?"
- "Pretend a patient on your unit right now is post-extubation, can't speak. The family has a 30-second voicemail of the patient from six months ago. Does what you just heard match what would matter to you and to the family?"
- "What about that demo would you push back on? What would a skeptical colleague say?"
- "What's missing for that to be useful in a real shift?"

### Probes for H-Co-Founder (palliative care + intensivists only)

Surfaced toward the end of the conversation, only if the rest of the conversation has been positive:

- "I'm building OwnVoice as an independent venture, not a research project. The clinical credibility piece is the part I can't do alone — I'm looking for a clinical co-founder. Is that something you'd want to learn more about?"
- "If you were building this with me, what would you most want OwnVoice to be in 3 years? What would you most *not* want it to be?"
- "Who else at your institution should I be talking to about this?"

## 6. Pivot-domain probes (the 5 conversations outside ICU)

These run on the same conversational arc but probe *whether the pivot domain has the funded-pain pattern that ICU may not.* The point is to know — at Day 90 — whether a pivot to outpatient palliative care, ALS, or pre-laryngectomy is a credible alternative if the ICU thesis fails.

For outpatient palliative care attendings:

- "What does your typical patient-with-progressive-disease look like, and what are the communication breakdowns you see late in the trajectory?"
- "What's your patient-pays vs. insurance-pays mix in your practice?"
- "If a patient you saw 6 months ago had voice-banked at the time of diagnosis, would that change how you'd be supporting them today?"
- "What AAC or voice-related tools do you currently recommend? What's the conversion rate from recommendation to actual use?"

For ALS clinic SLPs:

- "Walk me through the FYI Voice Preservation pathway from your end. Who actually does it? What's the take rate?"
- "Where does that pathway fail today? What patients are you wishing had banked but didn't?"
- "What would be different about a tool that worked from a 30-second voicemail rather than a structured 30-minute recording session?"

For head-and-neck oncology SLP:

- "How do you currently support patients in the pre-laryngectomy window for voice preservation?"
- "What's the typical insurance / patient-pay split on those services?"
- "Where in the pre-surgical timeline is the voice-banking conversation most useful?"

## 7. Demo guidance

The 60-second voice-clone demo is the most powerful single artifact you carry. Run it carefully:

- **Don't over-explain before pressing play.** "I want to show you something — drag-and-drop reference audio, on-device voice clone, takes about 30 seconds end-to-end. Watch what happens."
- **Watch the listener's face during playback.** That's the signal. The demo either lands viscerally or it doesn't.
- **Don't fill the silence after playback.** Let them respond first. Their unprompted reaction tells you more than any structured question.
- **Have 3–5 reference samples ready** of varying quality (clean read-speech, voicemail-quality, social-media-quality, multilingual). If the first sample lands, offer a second on a different quality tier — that tests the premium-tier hypothesis directly.
- **Never claim clinical outcomes.** Stick to the description: "this is a software communication tool; the patient or family controls the reference sample; nothing leaves the device after the clone is created [or: with the family's explicit consent for the cloud-enrolled premium tier]."

## 9. Day 30 self-review

After the first 10 interviews, sit down with the 10 memos and read them as a corpus. Ask:

- How many of the 10 had **strong-positive** signal on H-Existential?
- How many had **strong-negative** signal?
- Are the **objections clustering** around 1–2 themes, or are they scattered?
- Did any **surprises** appear that I should add to the probe list?
- Did the demo land more strongly than I expected, or less?
- Is the **co-founder pipeline** materializing (≥1 candidate engaged)?

If H-Existential is reading **strong-negative across 6+ of 10**, escalate the pivot-domain probes from "5 of 25" to "10 of remaining 15." Don't burn the remaining slots on a thesis that's already failed.

If the demo is landing **less strongly than expected**, prioritize voice-quality validation (collect more poor-reference samples, test premium tier) before further interviews — fixing the demo is higher-leverage than running more conversations with a weak demo.

## 10. Day 90 kill criteria

The decision gate at Day 90 reads the full corpus of memos against the following thresholds. Pre-commit to these *now*, in writing, so the goalposts can't move at Day 90.

**Strong positive signal (proceed to incorporation):**

- ≥6 of 20 ICU interviews show H-Existential STRONG POS (unprompted mention of voiceless-communication as a priority OR concrete budget envelope identified)
- ≥3 of 8 H-HRO probes returned STRONG POS (clear institutional-safety budget connection)
- ≥1 co-founder candidate seriously engaged (multiple meetings, willingness to discuss equity / IRB role)
- Demo landing emotionally (≥10 of 15 demoed interviewees show STRONG POS reaction)

**Strong negative signal (pivot or pause):**

- ≤2 of 20 ICU interviews show H-Existential POS, AND
- 0 procurement-relevant interviewees can name a current budget envelope this would fit, AND
- 0 of 5 has a recent purchase in this category

**Mixed signal (60-day extension):**

Anything between the above two thresholds. Don't incorporate yet; run another 60 days of focused experiments on whichever hypothesis is weakest.

## 11. Pivot scenarios (preview)

If discovery returns strong-negative signal on H-Existential, the credible pivots — informed by the 5 pivot-domain probes — are:

1. **Outpatient palliative care / hospice / home health.** Patient relationships are longer, advocacy is real, goals-of-care conversations are the daily work, SICG alignment becomes the headline use case. Different sales motion, different evidence base.
2. **ALS / MND specialty clinics.** Organized advocacy (ALSA), measurable progression, established voice-banking culture, the family-pays-with-time SKU set aside earlier.
3. **Post-laryngectomy / head-and-neck pre-surgical clinics.** Defined pre-surgical window, real demand, existing reimbursement mechanisms.
4. **License directly to AAC vendors at Year 2.** Skip the SaaS arc; sell the on-device + cloud voice-cloning stack as licensed IP to Tobii at Year 2 for $3–8M. Smaller exit, faster, lower execution risk.
5. **Bundle with delirium prevention.** Reposition OwnVoice as an ICU Liberation Bundle component where patient-driven communication is one input to delirium reduction (CMS-measured, reimbursement-linked). Different evidence base, different study design.

The pivot-domain probes give us enough signal at Day 90 to choose intelligently among these rather than pivoting blindly.
